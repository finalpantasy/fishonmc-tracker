What this compendium can prove

Location-table fields, official sprites, global rarity odds, variant bases, historical event odds, and published bait/lure effect text are source-backed. Ordinary location-pool per-fish odds are not published and are never manufactured here; conditional odds from specific historical events are labelled separately.

Automatic tracking

The local bridge prefers exact structured personal catches from the approval-gated collector and retains normalized PB notifications from latest.log as a safe fallback. Unknown fields remain unknown rather than guessed.
